## QUOTES-PROOF RULEBOOK

Hard rules. They gate the rubric, and the automated truth gate enforces them.

1. **Never invent quotes.** No quote attributed to That Boy Hi Hat, Black Lansky, or
   any CWI principal exists on record. If a story needs a voice, the contact is
   hp@cumulativeweb.com — the ask, not the invention.
2. **Never invent bios.** No narrative biography is on record. "Alternative rap,
   Frederick, MD" is an identity line, not a bio — write it exactly that short.
3. **Never invent coverage.** No press coverage is verified. Do not imply prior
   coverage; do not name outlets.
4. **Never claim lyrics.** No lyrics are published by the label. Do not narrate what
   a song "is about."
5. **One stat, dated.** Only Zooted Zone's plays may be cited, and only as
   "307K plays" with the observed date 2026-09-14. No growth language, ever —
   the display string stands alone. A fact with an observed date is a fact; a
   trajectory is a prediction.
6. **Campaigns are not placements.** Audiartist acceptances (#25818, #25820, #25821)
   are campaigns. New Rap Hits positions (#21, #30, #31) are placements.
7. **Failed verifications are not wins.** If a curator claim appears in copy, it
   must carry its FAILED VERIFICATION status from the Scoreboard Chip anomaly feed.
8. **Spelling is a fact.** "King Akeem" — the distributor misspelling was
   corrected by the owner. Get it right everywhere.

When a story needs something this kit doesn't have, the professional move is the
same as any wire desk: leave it out and file what you can prove.
