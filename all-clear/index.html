THE ALL-CLEAR

One fetch, one verdict: clear, conditional, or denied — for any track, for any use. Part of the Agent Deck gear line by Cumulative Web Inc.
What this does: any agent asks "can I use this track for X?" and gets a machine-readable verdict — clearconditionaldenied — with terms, territory, and contact, in one fetch. No login, no licensing portal, no "email us and wait two weeks." The verdicts are pre-signed by the rights holder, cacheable forever, and reasoned-over offline. What it is not: legal advice. Verdicts reflect the rights holder's stated position at the time of publication; terms can change.

THE VERDICT VOCABULARY

VerdictMeaning
