## Facts

- **[verified]** Anatomy indexed from 'Digital Anatomy of Four Music-Marketing Machines' research report (2026-10-03); per-fact evidence tiers and sources recorded in attrs.key_facts — _~/workspace/research_notes/music-marketing-machine-anatomy-20261003-1237/report.md_
- **[verified]** Monthly song-level opt-in (11th-last day of month, live the 1st of next month); auto-includes all track URIs/versions of a selected song — _https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/using-discovery-mode-in-spotify-for-artists/_
- **[verified]** Eligibility: >=3 eligible songs, >=25,000 monthly listeners, >=20 streams per song in Discovery Mode contexts in the last 28 days, plus 30 days on platform — _https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/using-discovery-mode-in-spotify-for-artists/_
- **[verified]** Release metadata rules: album/EP/single, <=4 main artists, full/split rights, no compilations/remasters/re-recordings/karaoke/soundalikes/white noise/spoken word; Artist Profile Protection approval; licensor allowlist (Amuse, CD Baby, CmdShft, DistroKid, EmuBands, EVEARA, Feiyr, Horus Music, LANDR, Lanzadera Music, MyMusic Sp, OFFstep, Rebeat Digital, recordJet, Repost Network, Soundrop, Stem, UnitedMasters, Venice Music, Vydia); no AI Persona badge — _https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/using-discovery-mode-in-spotify-for-artists/_
- **[verified]** Cost: $0 cash; 30% commission on recording royalties from streams of selected songs in Discovery Mode contexts only, deducted from future statements — _https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/using-discovery-mode-in-spotify-for-artists/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** At a ~$3-$5 per 1,000 streams royalty baseline, 1,000 DM streams net ~$2.10-$3.50 vs. $3-$5 — _https://www.pophatch.com/blog/spotify-discovery-mode_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Key subtlety: the 30% commission applies to DM-context streams whether or not the algorithm would have served the track there anyway - tracks with existing Radio/Autoplay traffic pay a discount on listening they already had — _https://www.pophatch.com/blog/spotify-discovery-mode_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Spotify claims: +50% saves, +44% playlist adds, +37% follows in the first month, and 31% of DM listeners brand-new to the artist — _https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/07/15/discovery-mode-on-spotify-should-artists-actually-use-it/_
- **[verified]** Spotify states it increases the likelihood of recommendation but does not guarantee it, and does not affect editorial playlists — _https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/using-discovery-mode-in-spotify-for-artists/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Flywheel: boosted Radio/Autoplay exposure -> new listeners -> on-demand/catalog streams at full royalty (only DM-context streams take the 30% cut) -> stronger algorithmic profile; industry analyses estimate 100-400% stream lifts during active campaigns — _https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/07/15/discovery-mode-on-spotify-should-artists-actually-use-it/_
  - lift estimates also via https://www.pophatch.com/blog/spotify-discovery-mode
- **[claimed_unverified]** The November 2025 'modern payola' class-action complaint alleges Discovery Mode's dynamic (Spotify monetizing its distribution bottleneck) — _https://notnoise.co/blog/spotify-marquee-vs-showcase-vs-discovery-mode_
