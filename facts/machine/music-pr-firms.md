## Facts

- **[observed]** Anatomy indexed from 'Digital Anatomy of Four Music-Marketing Machines' research report (2026-10-03); per-fact evidence tiers and sources recorded in attrs.key_facts — _~/workspace/research_notes/music-marketing-machine-anatomy-20261003-1237/report.md_
- **[verified]** Deliverables: press coverage - reviews, features, interviews, premieres, playlist/blog placements; a press kit/bio (often an upfront fee); monthly reporting of clippings with outlet reach; budget blog-PR firms promise volume (Independent Music Promotions: month-to-month $895, typically 12+ reviews/month) — _https://independentmusicpromotions.com/faq/_
  - publicist cost framing per https://djbooth.net/features/2023-02-20-costs-benefits-hiring-music-publicist
- **[claimed_unverified]** No ethical PR agency can guarantee independent editorial coverage — _https://www.swaggermagazine.com/style/how-much-does-pr-cost-2026/_
- **[verified]** Metrics: number of placements, outlet reach/readership, share of voice vs. competitors, sentiment, message pull-through; legacy metric AVE (advertising value equivalency) still auto-calculated by tools like Muck Rack but rejected by AMEC's Barcelona Principles 4.0 and banned by CIPR (2017) — _https://muckrack.com/blog/2024/05/14/pr-metrics-to-measure/_
  - AVE rejection per https://collabstr.com/blog/earned-media-value
- **[claimed_unverified]** Measurement guidance: replace AVE with sentiment trajectory, message pull-through, share of voice, and coverage quality weighted by outlet relevance - a big number in the wrong outlet is worthless — _https://delve.news/post/ave-is-dead-what-your-pr-report-should-measure-instead/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Media database is rented: Cision (1M+ journalist contacts, full PR suite - database, distribution, monitoring, analytics; expensive, annual contracts, complex) or Muck Rack (journalist profiles, pitch tracking, monitoring, AI list building; added press-release distribution in 2025) — _https://gist.github.com/williamhsu/b5576d9b0d5a137ee2fc71135f8f6888_
  - Muck Rack model per https://startupgrowthguide.com/comprehensive-muck-rack-case-study-the-growth-blueprint/
- **[claimed_unverified]** Pitching engine is human labor: personalized pitches, follow-up sequences, relationship management - the core labor; Muck Rack's pitch tracking + AI list building automate the plumbing, not the pitch itself — _report section D4 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Reactive pitching: HARO (now Connectively - free, journalists come to you, but reactive/competitive/no targeting) and paid feeds: Qwoted (free: 2 pitches/mo with 2-hour delay; Pro $149/mo), Featured.com (free: 3 submissions/mo; Pro $99/mo); the 2-hour free-tier delay is 'the whole game' for serious reactive PR — _https://subscribepr.com/blog/how-much-does-a-newsjacking-program-cost/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Wire distribution: press releases pushed to newswires for indexable pickup (low authority, but persistent entity corroboration) — _https://subscribepr.com/blog/how-much-does-a-newsjacking-program-cost/_
  - evidence gap: exact current per-release wire pricing (PR Newswire/Business Wire) for music announcements not pulled from live pricing pages - could not verify
- **[claimed_unverified]** Pricing: music publicists charge flat fees from 'a few hundred to several thousand dollars monthly' (DJBooth); 2026 PR broadly: boutique agencies $3,000-$10,000/mo; freelancers $1,500-$3,500/mo retainer or $150-$500/hr; mid-size agencies $10,000-$25,000/mo; projects $5,000-$25,000 — _https://www.swaggermagazine.com/style/how-much-does-pr-cost-2026/_
  - freelancer rates per https://subscribepr.com/blog/should-a-small-business-hire-a-freelance-publicist/
- **[claimed_unverified]** Unit economics: the retainer buys labor hours - at ~$333/hr billed for a senior AE, a $3,000/mo boutique retainer ~= ~9-15 hours of real work monthly (blended with junior time); tools are the smallest line item (Muck Rack $5,000+/user/yr, Qwoted Pro $149/mo); margin comes from leverage - one publicist runs multiple retainers — _https://subscribepr.com/blog/should-a-small-business-hire-a-freelance-publicist/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Retainer hour-split model (~20% strategy/angle work, 30% media-list research + pitch drafting, 30% sending + follow-ups + relationship maintenance, 20% monitoring + reporting) is a model, not a quote - firms do not publish hour splits — _report section D7 (synthesis)_
  - treat as a model, not a quote
- **[claimed_unverified]** Infrastructure: the PR firm owns almost no infrastructure - it is a labor + rented-data + relationships business; journalist data lives on the vendors' servers under annual SaaS contracts (when the contract ends, the data stays with the vendor); coverage artifacts live on publishers' domains — _report section D5 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Relationship data (who answers, what they cover, response history, warm introductions) in the firm's CRM/pitch logs is the closest thing to proprietary data in PR — _report section D3 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Genuinely proprietary: almost nothing technical - the moats are (a) the journalist relationship graph in the publicist's head/CRM, (b) taste and news judgment, (c) reputation that gets emails opened; none of these are purchasable software — _report section D8 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Rebuildable at zero cost - nearly the entire stack: media-list building via free sources (outlet mastheads, journalist Twitter/X bios, Substack directories, Spotify editorial team pages); pitching via any email client with a tracking spreadsheet; press-kit assembly with free design tools; monitoring via Google Alerts + free Talkwalker alerts; reactive pitching via Connectively (free tier) and Featured.com (free tier); paid databases buy speed and completeness, not capability - a determined indie team can hand-build a 100-contact genre media list in a weekend — _report section D8 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** What you cannot replicate for free: the publicist's open-rate (relationships) and the judgment of which 10 of 100 contacts matter - that is the actual product; everything else is workflow anyone can copy — _report section D8 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Amplification: press quotes become ad creative and pitch social proof (raising Meta auction quality scores); reviews/placements drive search visibility and Wikipedia/knowledge-panel eligibility; editorial co-signs feed Spotify editorial playlist consideration indirectly (editors read press); coverage gives playlist curators and radio programmers a reason to say yes — _report section D6 (synthesis)_
