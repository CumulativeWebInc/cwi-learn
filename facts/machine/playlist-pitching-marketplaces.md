- **[observed]** Anatomy indexed from 'Digital Anatomy of Four Music-Marketing Machines' research report (2026-10-03); per-fact evidence tiers and sources recorded in attrs.key_facts — _~/workspace/research_notes/music-marketing-machine-anatomy-20261003-1237/report.md_
- **[verified]** What is sold is a review, not a placement: curator consideration + written feedback; placement is the curator's independent decision - the legal/ToS line that keeps them clear of payola rules — _https://playlistpush.com/spotify-playlists-promotion?ref=playlistpush.com_
- **[claimed_unverified]** SubmitHub: premium credits ~$0.80-$1.00 each (100 for $80; bulk discounts); 1-3 credits per curator typical, 4-credit top tier new in 2026; typical 30-50 submission campaign = $30-$80; credits never expire; free Standard credits (2 per 4 hours) for testing — _https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/submithub-review-2026_
  - pricing also via https://www.dontpayfull.com/at/submithub.com
- **[claimed_unverified]** SubmitHub premium response SLA: SOURCES DISAGREE - 48 hours (submitlink.io) vs 72 hours (musicinminnesota, letssubmit); likely tier-dependent after the 2026 tier rework — _https://www.submitlink.io/compare/submithub_
  - evidence gap: flag rather than resolve; see https://www.musicinminnesota.com/best-spotify-playlist-service/
- **[claimed_unverified]** SubmitHub approvals: average ~24% all outlets, ~29% Spotify playlisters (Dynamoi citing 2025 data); free premium-credit routes: Hot or Not, Verified Reviewers, referrals — _https://dynamoi.com/vs/groover-vs-submithub_
- **[verified]** Groover: 2 Grooviz = EUR 2 per contact; top-tier 4-6 Grooviz; packs from EUR 46 with up to 20-24% volume discount; practical minimum ~EUR 100 (50 curators as Groover recommends); guaranteed listening + written feedback within 7 days or credits back; 90% reply rate; also connects to labels/radio/journalists — _https://groover.co_
- **[verified]** Playlist Push: from $280; example $630 to reach 79 playlists; average ~$450; price varies by genre/curators; genre-matching automation; purchases final, unused budget -> account credit; Artist Protection Fund auto-refunds unfulfilled reviews; detailed report 30 days after campaign if Spotify for Artists is connected — _https://playlistpush.com/spotify-playlists-promotion?ref=playlistpush.com_
- **[claimed_unverified]** SoundCampaign: from $80; Sept 2026 estimator: $150 -> ~17 projected curator reviews; pay only per completed review; budget can split across Spotify, TikTok creators, and Meta ads — _https://magneticmag.com/2026/10/top-5-online-music-promotion-platforms-to-grow-your-audience-in-2026/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Two-sided unit economics: artist pays ~$1-9 per human review; curator earns $1-$5 per review (SubmitHub, by tier/traffic); the platform keeps the spread plus breakage on unused credits/balances; curator payouts are the platform's COGS; margin is in aggregation - placements cannot be bought without violating Spotify's ToS, so the sellable unit is guaranteed human attention — _https://music.loop.fans/blog/submithub-curator_
  - curator $1-$5 figure per https://music.loop.fans/blog/submithub-curator
- **[claimed_unverified]** Playlist Push / SoundCampaign curator payout rates: only dated (2019, ~$0.75/review) or third-party figures; current platform-side payout schedules are not public — _https://medium.com/@colortheory/playlist-push-revisited-12ed8c6b10ef_
  - evidence gap: could not verify
- **[claimed_unverified]** Structural comparison: stream-target services instead sell a defined stream target (e.g., from $129 one-time or $149-$999/mo) verifiable in Spotify for Artists - headline prices are not comparable across the two structures — _https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/chartlex-vs-submithub-vs-playlist-push-vs-soundcampaign-2026_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Trust & safety: SubmitHub free AI Song Checker (labels/blocks likely-AI tracks at 85%+), Groover self-declare checkbox + curator opt-out tags, Playlist Push monitoring for fake/inactive followers and multiple accounts, curator tiering with demotion for lapsed standards (SubmitHub 2026: 4-credit top tier is earned, 30-day grace before demotion) — _https://letssubmit.com/blog/pro-tips/submithub-vs-groover-vs-playlist-push-vs-letssubmit-playlist-pitching-costs-2026_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Matching: genre-tag similarity + historical acceptance data route tracks to curators (Playlist Push, SoundCampaign); filter sliders and genre-match scores assist manual selection (SubmitHub); none publish a true ML model - it is rules + historical stats — _https://playlistpush.com/spotify-playlists-promotion?ref=playlistpush.com_
  - manual-selection details per https://magneticmag.com/2026/10/top-5-online-music-promotion-platforms-to-grow-your-audience-in-2026/
- **[claimed_unverified]** Data moat: all submission, decision, feedback, pricing-tier, and reputation data lives on each platform's own servers; artists see only their own dashboards, curators only their queues; the cross-artist acceptance-rate dataset and curator reliability scores are the platforms' private moat; marketplaces have no independent stream measurement - downstream verification happens on Spotify's infrastructure via Spotify for Artists — _report section C5 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** ROI logic: independent playlist placements generate real streams -> saves/follows -> feed Spotify's algorithmic systems (Release Radar, Discover Weekly, Radio); paid curator access buys seeds for algorithmic compounding; low-quality/botted playlists produce streams Spotify may discount or penalize - manipulation can get music removed (Playlist Push's own warning) — _https://www.yougrowpromo.com/pages/meta-ads_
  - penalty warning per https://playlistpush.com/spotify-playlists-promotion?ref=playlistpush.com
- **[claimed_unverified]** Feedback as product: even declined submissions return written curator feedback - market intelligence on why a track isn't landing, reused across campaigns — _https://magneticmag.com/2026/10/top-5-online-music-promotion-platforms-to-grow-your-audience-in-2026/_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Genuinely proprietary: the vetted curator network (thousands of relationships, historical acceptance/reliability data, tiering) and the SLA/refund trust infrastructure - you cannot conjure 3,000 responsive curators with track records; the platforms' cross-artist dataset is their compounding data moat — _report section C8 (synthesis)_
- **[claimed_unverified]** Rebuildable at zero cost: curator discovery via Spotify playlist search + Instagram/TikTok + blog hunting (this is what SubmitHub's directory productizes); outreach via spreadsheet CRM + templated personalized emails + tracking sheet; SLA guarantee as personal follow-up cadence; genre-match filtering by manual listening - DIY costs time instead of $1-$9/review; an indie team with 10-20 hours can replicate a $100 SubmitHub campaign's mechanics (but not instant access to curators who already trust the platform's queue) — _report section C8 (synthesis)_
