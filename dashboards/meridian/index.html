⚡ Meridian Mind — Swift vs Deliberate

⚡ Meridian Swift

Rapid AI analysis for high-volume scanning. Same 1.5B model, same weights — one sharp analyst, no deliberation overhead.

Speed: ~25–45s per query
Best for: routine gap analysis, daily scans, bulk content review

◈ Meridian Deliberate

Full three-reasoner deliberation with dissent capture. Explorer, critic, and pragmatist debate; a rotating judge synthesizes with minority views preserved.

Speed: ~75–95s per query
Best for: high-stakes decisions, strategy calls, contested queries
