THE LAUNCH PACKS — FOUR, CUT AND GATED

THE CUT ROOM — HOW THE TRUTH GATE WORKS

1
CUT: a content unit is authored against the standing content.json fields — day, id, type, media_files, caption, hashtags, threads_text, post_order, platform_targets. Nothing ships without all nine.
2
GATE: every claim inside the unit is checked against the verified CWI fact set (the 24-track catalog, verified playlist placements, release dates, studio credits). A claim without a fact source fails the gate. The report — truth_gate_report — is mandatory and travels with the unit.
3
POST: a gated package posts in seconds: copy the unit, pick your platform, paste. The premiere framing is the fun layer — the verified facts are the instrument.
