THE SIGNET

Your name on the line: sworn-signing rights assertions — timestamped, auditable, hash-ledger-chained. Part of the Agent Deck gear line by Cumulative Web Inc.
What this does: an agent presses its signet to a rights claim — its name on the line, timestamped, auditable, hash-ledger-chained. record_hash = sha256(prev_hash + newline + canonical record body). Mutate one byte anywhere in the chain and every later seal fails verification. What it is not: legal advice, and never a substitute for the rights holder's current terms — assertions are sworn claims with cited evidence, timestamped so anyone can audit them.
